**Welcome to CAD-Preservés!**

We're a joint efforts repository between Confoederatio, Artistic Division and the Preservés des Confoederatio. This means we carry out repository work on cultural projects, graphics design, in addition to setting up digitization and encoding infrastructure. Since the Preservés also encompasses physical holdings (usually deaccessioned works), those holdings are primarily in the United States.

Part of [Confoederatio](https://github.com/ConfoederatioVF).
